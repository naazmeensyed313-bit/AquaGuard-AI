AquaGuard AI — Concept Note & Lean Canvas

AI-Powered Water Quality Screening & Regulatory Decision Support System | Aligned with UN SDG 6

Project Concept Note

1. Problem

  • Lab Latency: Traditional multi-barrier laboratory testing takes 3 to 7 days, delaying contamination alerts.
  • High Testing Costs: Complete lab assays for every wellhead or rural distribution point are economically unfeasible.
  • Field Ambiguity: Field technicians capture sensor telemetry (pH, turbidity, TDS) but lack instant triage guidance.
  • Black-Box Trust: Generic ML models provide opaque scores without explainability or regulatory benchmark alignment.

4. Solution

  • Dual-Layer Screening: Calibrated GBDT ML + automated BIS IS 10500 & WHO compliance mapping.
  • Explainable AI (SHAP): Real-time feature attributions explaining which parameters drove risk elevation.
  • High-Throughput Batch: Drag-and-drop CSV validation scoring up to 500 field samples in seconds.

8. Key Metrics

  • Triage Turnaround: < 200ms per sample screening.
  • Model Reliability: ROC-AUC 0.924, Accuracy 84.0%, Brier Score 0.095.
  • Prioritization Ratio: 60% reduction in unnecessary statutory lab dispatches.

3. Unique Value Proposition

  • Rapid Field Triage in Seconds: Converts raw 9-parameter water probe readings into calibrated risk bands and actionable decisions.
  • Explainable Over Black-Box: Transparent SHAP bar charts prove why a sample was flagged.
  • Responsible AI by Design: Explicitly non-diagnostic; refuses false drinkability claims and prioritizes laboratory verification.
  • Zero LLM Hallucinations: Purely deterministic geochemical ML pipeline.

9. Unfair Advantage

  • Dual-Engine Architecture: Simultaneously checks statistical geochemical patterns and formal legal BIS standards.
  • Zero-Config Deployment: Single-terminal runner, offline-ready SQLite session persistence, and local mock fallback.

5. Channels

  • Government Water Boards: Public health surveillance (Jal Jeevan Mission / CPCB).
  • Municipal Utilities: Drinking water distribution & treatment grid integration.
  • Open APIs: Direct RESTful ingestion for IoT water sensor gateways.

2. Customer Segments

  • Public Health & Municipal Bodies: Urban water supply departments monitoring distribution grids.
  • Rural Water Missions: Field operators managing borewells and community tanks with handheld probes.
  • Industrial Effluent Monitors: Environmental audit teams screening factory discharge compliance.
  • Environmental NGOs: Citizen-science and watershed conservation teams.

7. Cost Structure

  • Compute & Cloud Infrastructure: Lightweight FastAPI + SQLite backend hosting and CDN asset delivery.
  • ML Pipeline & Calibration: Retraining and validating GBDT models on regional geochemical survey datasets.
  • Regulatory Maintenance: Continual updating of Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS IS 10500) and WHO benchmark tables.
  • Quality Assurance & Field Auditing: Verification of sensor telemetry variance across seasonal shifts.

6. Revenue Streams & Impact Model

  • SaaS Subscription for Water Utilities: Tiered enterprise licenses for regional watershed surveillance dashboards and automated batch processing.
  • API Ingestion Licensing: Pay-per-query or monthly tiers for IoT sensor manufacturers and hardware logger telemetry gateways.
  • Accredited Lab Referral Integration: Digital dispatch and split-sample tracking partnerships with certified testing laboratories.
  • Social Impact (UN SDG 6): Accelerates universal access to monitored water sources by lowering the barrier to early risk detection.
Educational & Decision-Support Notice: AquaGuard AI is a screening triage tool and does not certify water as safe or potable. It is designed to assist environmental surveillance and prioritize samples for statutory, accredited laboratory verification.
